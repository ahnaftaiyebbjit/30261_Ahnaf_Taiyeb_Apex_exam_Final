Write the Validation Rule formula that prevents an Opportunity from being saved as Closed Won when
Amount is blank or not greater than zero.

AND(
    IS_PICKVALUE('Closed Won', True),
    OR(
        IS_BLANK(Amount),
        IS_TRUE(Amount < 0)
    )
